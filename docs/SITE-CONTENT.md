## 7. Sea Level Rise Page (`/sea-level-rise`)

### Research Focus
As sea level rises, so does groundwater. Preferential pathways: utility trenches, leaky sewer pipes, stormdrains, buried stream channels.

### Technology & Sensors
- IntelliSense AWARE Flood Sensors (real-time water sensors with flood alerts)
- Solinst continuous data loggers (shallow well groundwater measurements)
- SmartCover sewer/stormwater sensors

### Evidence of Emergent Groundwater
- Wicking groundwater between concrete slabs and up concrete walls
- Numerous sump pumps (shallow groundwater ~2 ft below surface)
- Water discharge from overflow pipes
- Curb drains throughout neighborhoods

### Pilot Study Area
- Location: Tamalpais Valley, California (filled wetlands, San Francisco Bay Area)
- Proposed: Small-diameter groundwater monitoring wells, stilling wells
- Data: Compare tidal, rain, creek/wetland elevations for groundwater movement analysis
- Gap addressed: Current SLR mitigation doesn't account for groundwater emerging through preferential pathways

### Community Participation
- Invitation to document emergent groundwater during "King Tides"
- Photo submission with date, time, location details

### Downloads
- Research proposal: Prediction of Sanitary Sewer Overflow Conditions and Mitigation Using Remote Telemetry
- "Predicting Emergent Groundwater" document
- BACWA abstracts and meeting notes
